After playing the [Jet Lag](https://jetlagthegame.com/) [Home Game](https://store.nebula.tv/products/jet-lag-the-game-hide-and-seek-transit-game) a few times with friends, I set out to make a physical map I could have printed to use while playing (instead of being on my phone all the time). After a few hours spent learning how to use [QGIS](https://qgis.org/) and some perusing of the NYC & NY State Open Data platforms, I had my map!

Some features you'll find on this map making it ideal for playing Jet Lag: The Game - Hide + Seek
* All Hiding Zones (Option between including or excluding MTA Rail) marked
* Coastline "countour lines" at 0.25 mile increments
* Voronoi tessellation of NYC's airports
* "Attractions" & Parks marked  

I maintain a personal version of the map that swaps out the "Notes" field for the list of questions; but opted not to publish that to respect Jet Lag's hard work on the home game (which I highly recommend).
